I have been designing and implementing software since I was a child. When I was twelve years old, my father brought home a PC from his work at IBM. I wasn't too happy about the fact that he forgot to order it with a color screen and games.
He refused my request to upgrade the hardware and suggested that I program a game myself. So I learned Quickbasic and dived into the world of programming.
I wasn't too successful back then, but it laid the foundation for my later studies of computer science at university.

I successfully completed my degree in 1999 and started working for the company where I had written my thesis.
When the dot-com crisis hit, I lost my job and decided to start as a freelancer. 
A few years later, I founded my current company together with a former colleague and friend.

Since then, I have worked on numerous projects, written millions of lines of code, and watched ideas and concepts come and go.
I've held trainings, spoken at conferences, and helped companies build robust, testable, bug-free software that just works.

And I have always loved what I do. ❤️
